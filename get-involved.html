How it works

Inside a ten-week project.

Every engagement follows the same arc—from a company’s real problem to a working prototype in the sponsor’s hands.

Weeks 1–2

Kickoff & Discovery

The team meets its company sponsor, defines the problem, agrees on success metrics, and secures data access.

Weeks 3–4

Users & Data

Students explore the data, research approaches, and frame a technical plan with their faculty mentor.

Weeks 5–8

Build Sprints

Working in agile sprints, the team prototypes and reviews progress with company stakeholders each week.

Week 9

Documentation & Delivery

The team prepares to hand off final deliverables, including clear documentation of both what was and was not done.

Week 10

Presentation & Final Demo

The team exhibits their work in a final presentation to project stakeholders at the company.
