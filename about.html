About the program

Learning from experience in AI development.

Chicago LEAD is a joint university initiative that turns classroom knowledge into real business impact—by putting student teams to work on live AI projects for Chicago companies.

Why LEAD exists

AI is transforming every sector, but companies keep hitting the same wall: talent that pairs technical skill with real business judgment is hard to find. Plenty of developers know the models—far fewer have managed a client, scoped an ambiguous problem, or shipped something a business will actually use.

Founded by Northwestern University and Northeastern Illinois University with support from the National Science Foundation and the Illinois Innovation Voucher Program, LEAD closes that gap with experiential learning. Cross-university student teams work ten-week projects sourced from local companies, guided by faculty advisors with both industry and development experience.

The result is talent that arrives job-ready—and a growing applied-AI ecosystem that positions Chicago as a national hub for turning innovation into measurable impact.

What makes it work

Real projects

Every brief comes from a Chicago company with a genuine problem, real data, and real users—not a case study.

Cross-university teams

Teams of three to five students from different schools learn to collaborate the way real workplaces do.

Faculty mentorship

Mentors with industry and development backgrounds coach students through the technical and the professional.

Business outcomes

Success is measured in working prototypes and business value, not grades—so students build judgment, not just code.

Frequently asked
How long is a project?

Each project runs ten weeks, with the option to extend when both the team and the company see continued value.

Who can participate as a student?

Students from our four partner universities across the Chicago area, working in cross-university teams of three to five.

What does it take for a company to sponsor a project?

A real problem worth addressing, access to the relevant data, and a stakeholder who can meet with the team through the engagement. Reach out and we'll help scope a good first project!

What kinds of AI projects are a good fit?

Students on LEAD projects have built data pipelines, content authoring platforms, research assistants, time-saving automations, recommendation systems, and more. We work with clients ahead of project launch to make sure projects are appropriate shaped and scoped to fit both student and client needs.

When do sessions run?

Sessions run each academic term. See Get Involved to join the next cohort or sponsor a project.
